## The strategy

Every conversation from now to December is one move in the same sequence. Each party is sold on what the others bring, so each yes becomes the proof for the next conversation.

1. **Golfzon. Sell the bays to get the partnership.** A 272-bay programme in Virgin Active and Planet Fitness gets us in the room with Golfzon's global head. We leave with chain pricing, a reference design and an agreement to talk about Get Lucky mode inside Golfzon's software.
2. **Planet Fitness (and Virgin Active). Sell the concept.** Golf zones at zero capex, financed by RMB, run by Get Lucky, and backed by Ernie Els.
3. **RMB. Sell the finance.** Asset finance for the bays, backed by concessions with Virgin Active and Planet Fitness, Golfzon pricing, a Santam-insured shot and the bay model.
4. **Icons. Sell the R10 million hole-in-one** at Icons Cup South Africa, fully underwritten and at no cost to Icons. Get Lucky becomes the Official Hole-in-One Partner.
5. **Santam. Sell the global book.** An app backed by Ernie Els, running globally on Golfzon and launched on the Icons broadcast. First the SA foundation cover, then the R10m quote.
6. **Icons Cup, Fancourt, 11–13 December. Close.** Sign Ernie as founding partner there, and announce Get Lucky going global: the app, integrated with Golfzon, and Virgin Golf and Planet Golf bays from 2027.

> **The principle** Nobody signs first on their own. Each party signs **subject to** the others: Golfzon pricing subject to the order, the gym deals subject to finance, RMB subject to the concessions. Then they all convert together. Sequence the *conditional* yeses fast, and the firm ones follow in one closing stack.

## Breaking the chicken-and-egg

Each party wants to see someone else commit first:
- Golfzon wants a firm order.
- RMB wants signed concessions and a Golfzon quote.
- Planet Fitness and Virgin Active want zero capex, which means RMB.

Break it with **conditional commitments in the right order**:

1. **Golfzon (25 Sep): indicative chain pricing and a reference design, "subject to finance and concessions".** That gives RMB and PF a real number.
2. **Planet Fitness (2 Oct): an LOI for phase 1 (10 clubs, 20 Signature bays, 2027), "subject to finance and the reference design".** That gives RMB a signed counterparty and Virgin Active a precedent.
3. **RMB (October): an indicative term sheet** on the PF LOI plus the Golfzon quote. Virgin Active is the upside, not the condition.
4. **Close together (January–February 2027):** the firm Golfzon order, RMB credit approval and signed concessions in one closing stack. Phase-1 installs follow.

Santam and Icons run on a separate track from the bays and move faster:
- **The SA cover opens the app.**
- **The R10m quote makes the Icons prize real.**

Both must be in writing before the October announcement.

## Meeting brief: Golfzon, Friday 25 September

**Who:** Sean Pyun, President and CEO of Golfzon America and Head of Global Business. He moved the Global Business Division to Chantilly, Virginia in March 2026, and he builds partnerships with operators rather than making one-off sales. Check the spelling in every calendar invite and email: **Sean Pyun**, not "Shaun Payn".

**Objective:** Leave as Golfzon's gym-chain partner in Southern Africa, with the software conversation open.

**Open with:** "We're bringing Golfzon into South Africa's two biggest gym chains, financed, operated and booked like padel. We want Golfzon as the technology partner, and we want to put a Golfzon prize product on every one of your bays worldwide."

**What we bring:**
- The programme:
  - Virgin Active: 200 bays in 130 clubs.
  - Planet Fitness: 72 bays in 42 clubs.
  - Phased 2027–29, about $11M of hardware at list.
- The finance: RMB asset finance at 90%, with Get Lucky putting in 10% equity.
- The insured shot: live on real courses, Santam carrying the risk. The app is built end to end, with a sealed evidence chain for every claim.
- Content and a league engine: 30 partner courses to scan into the Golfzon library, and 600 golf days a year.
- Icons Cup South Africa at Fancourt, broadcast to 150+ countries, where Get Lucky owns a par-3 with a R10m prize.

**The asks, in order:**
1. **Hardware:** chain pricing on the three bay types, a reference design for a two-bay zone, and an SA service partner.
2. **Software:** agreement in principle to scope **Get Lucky mode** inside Golfzon, with signed shot events, member log-in and NASMO video.
3. **Data:** an NDA covering ace-rate history by hole and distance (for Santam's pricing), and bay counts by country.
4. **Icons:** a Golfzon demo bay in the Fancourt fan zone playing the Get Lucky hole. That puts Golfzon on the broadcast in December.
5. **Next step:** a named owner at Golfzon, a pricing date, and Chantilly (Q4) or the PGA Show (January).

**Do not:**
- Put Ernie in writing. If he comes up, say: "We're finalising a major-winning founding partner, and you'll see him at Fancourt."
- Call the gym bays an order. Say "the programme we're rolling out".
- Send the Virgin Active pitch site.
- Let a distributor own the relationship.

> **Critical path** Icons was told in August that the R10m prize was "secured… fully underwritten". Santam received the quote request on 22 September. That way the Icons prize holds whatever Santam decides, and Santam keeps first right to be named on it.
