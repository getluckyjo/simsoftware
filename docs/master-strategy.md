## The plan on one page

**The mission.** Get Lucky turns the hole-in-one into a product: one insured swing, a life-changing prize, paid on the spot. It sells on every par-3 a golfer stands on, whether that is a real course, a gym bay or any simulator, anywhere in the world.

**Three engines**, each with its own route to market:
1. **The app on real courses.** The Get Lucky app sells insured swings on every par-3 in the country: 95 courses and 385 par-3s are loaded today. South Africa comes first, then the US and Europe, with no physical build per course.
2. **The bays.** Virgin Golf and Planet Golf: 272 Golfzon bays in South Africa's two biggest gym chains. They are financed by RMB and run by Get Lucky. That is a property-style cash-flow business with the insured shot on every bay.
3. **The simulator software.** "Get Lucky mode" inside Golfzon's software, proven on the gym bays and then rolled out to Golfzon's ~13,000 locations. A Get Lucky app for other simulator brands follows.

**One insurance book** sits under all three: Santam through Indwe in South Africa, one-off cover for the R10m Icons prize, and global cover for the US and Europe.

**One brand and one launch.** Get Lucky is the master brand. The public launch is Icons Cup South Africa at Fancourt, 11–13 December 2026, with a R10m hole-in-one on the Get Lucky hole. Ernie Els signs there as founding partner.

```stats
**R600m** | app entry revenue over 36 months (insurance model, base case)
**R261m** | bay revenue a year at maturity, R151m EBITDA (VA + PF, 2031)
**$16M** | simulator software revenue in 2029 (Golfzon base case)
**R144m** | premium to Santam over 36 months from the app
**11 Dec** | the launch: Icons Cup, The Links at Fancourt
```

> **The rule that holds it together** The plan must work **without** the amplifiers (Ernie, Icons, Virgin Active), just more slowly. It **cannot** work without the three load-bearing pieces: Santam cover, RMB finance and a simulator platform. Each load-bearing piece gets a fallback. Each amplifier gets pursued hard, but never promised early.

## Engine 1: the app on real courses

**What it is.** One swing at one par-3, bought in the app before you tee off, with the prize insured by Santam. There are no subscriptions and no unlimited swings. The claim is verified with sealed video, GPS, timestamps, witnesses and club confirmation.

**The economics** (Get Lucky Insurance Model, 36 months):
- **Ladder:** $1→$500, $5→$2,500, $10→$10,000, $50→$50,000 and $100→$100,000. The blended entry is $17.
- **Premium:** 24% of every entry for 100% cover. Loss ratio 31.9%; break-even at 2.5× amateur ace odds.
- **Volume:** 1.9m insured swings. Entry revenue of $1.6M in year 1, $9.0M in year 2 and $21.8M in year 3, **$32.4M (about R600m) in total**.
- **What Get Lucky keeps:** 76% of each entry before payments, marketing and operations.

**How it grows:**
- **SA:** the 30-course network and the Top 100 courses. The October "Back an Icon" campaign drives registrations before December.
- **US and Europe:** 42M golfers on about 23,000 courses. The app goes to market with no build per course. Paid play opens only where legal opinions allow: the US dominant-factor states first, then the UK after counsel's opinion.
- **The growth assumption to watch:** the model's year-2 and year-3 volumes lean on a **200,000-player "Ernie Partner Network"** (PGA, Golf Breaks, YGT, 100 partner courses) and on **global markets opening in year 3**. Those volumes depend on Ernie signing and on those partnerships.

## Engine 2: the bays (Virgin Golf and Planet Golf)

**What it is.** Get Lucky buys, installs and runs Golfzon bays inside gym clubs under 10-year concessions:
- The gym gives the room and the members, and takes 20% of every rand.
- RMB finances 90% of capex, and Get Lucky puts in 10% equity.
- Revenue comes from bay hire booked like padel, a R299 a month golf add-on, leagues, a naming sponsor and the insured shot.

## Engine 3: the simulator software (Golfzon)

**What it is.**
- Golfzon builds "Get Lucky mode" into its commercial software. The Collection of famous-inspired par-3s is played with the tee locked to the prize tier's distance.
- Golfzon sends signed shot events to Get Lucky.
- Get Lucky runs the wallet, identity checks, insurance, claims and payouts. That is the same back end as the app.

**Stages:**
- The gym bays from 2027.
- Golfzon's US venues in 2028.
- Golfzon's global network from 2029: paid play where legal, free play with sponsor prizes elsewhere.
- Get Lucky's own lightweight app for TrackMan and Uneekor venues after that, which is also the fallback.

**Economics (base case, 2029):**
- 2,500 connected bays, 80 paid shots per bay per month.
- **About $16.4M revenue and $5.8M contribution** after a 15% Golfzon share, which is still to be negotiated.
- First-year build $0.8–1.2M.

Full detail is in the research report.

**Two ladders, one book.** Real courses and simulators have different odds, so they need different prize ladders:
- **Real courses (app):** amateur odds are about 1 in 12,500, so the ladder can pay 500–1,000×. Example: $10 wins $10,000.
- **Simulators:** aces come about 1.8× more often, around 1 in 7,000 on Golfzon, so the ladder is leaner and prizes scale with tee distance. Example: $10 wins $5,000. The $100K tier is gated.

Santam should hear this from us first. It shows we price each surface on its own odds.

## Capital plan

**What needs funding:**
- **Bays:** R25.1m of equity (10%), plus the 2027 cash low point in the model (–R13.3m across both chains). RMB funds the other 90% of capex.
- **Simulator software:** $0.8–1.2M (about R15–22m) in the first year.
- **App launch:** marketing for the October–December campaign, legal opinions (SA first, about $70–170K for SA plus the US), and insurance set-up.
- **Icons:** the rig, the tracer and camera verification. The prize itself is insured, and the premium is offset by the rights package Santam buys into.

**How to fund it, in order:**
1. **Now to December: bridge on the current balance sheet and partner money.** Offset the Icons premium with the Santam brand-side rights package. Get sponsor revenue from named holes.
2. **After Fancourt: raise once, at the new valuation.** With Santam cover, Golfzon, a signed gym chain, RMB and Ernie all real, the equity story is worth more in January than in October. Consider offering Ernie equity or a founding-partner share instead of cash. Size the raise to cover bay equity, the simulator build and 18 months of US entry.
3. **Debt for the bays.** RMB, with Golfzon vendor or lease finance and a second bank as alternatives.
