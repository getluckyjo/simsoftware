**What each paid shot could earn Golfzon.** At the planning case of 80 paid shots per bay per month and a $9 average entry, a bay takes about $720 a month in paid shots. At a 15% Golfzon share:
- Golfzon earns about **$108 per bay per month, around $1,300 a year**. That is on hardware already sold, with no new kit.
- The venue earns about **$144 per bay per month** at its 20% share.

For comparison, commercial Golfzon venues pay about $2–4K per bay per year for software today. **Use these numbers to show the size of the prize, not as a rate offer.**
