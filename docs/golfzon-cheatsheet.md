## Open (60 seconds)
"Golfzon moving global business to Chantilly told us where to come. This isn't a dealer conversation. Get Lucky sells golf's most exciting moment, insured, at 30 courses with 10,000 paid entries and Santam on the risk. We're bringing simulators into SA's two biggest gym chains: **200 bays proposed to Virgin Active and 72 to Planet Fitness**, RMB-financed and run by us. **We're choosing the platform now, and we want it to be Golfzon.**"

## The turn: "Ask us in Virginia"
"Your simulators measure the ace better than any camera on a tee. Build a **Get Lucky mode** into Golfzon. You lock the tee and send us the shot; we run the wallet, identity checks, the Santam cover, claims and payouts. Every bay gets a new paid moment, and **the venue and Golfzon both earn on every shot**. Korea already runs ace pools. We take it everywhere else."

## The five asks
1. **Chain pricing** for 3 bay types × 3 phases (indicative, subject to finance).
2. **Reference designs** (2-bay and 1-bay) and an **SA service partner**.
3. **Scoping Get Lucky mode**: a named person, 4–6 weeks.
4. **NDA**: aggregated ace data and bay counts by country.
5. **A Golfzon bay at Fancourt**, playing the Get Lucky hole.

Then lock: **Chantilly in October** and the **PGA Show, 26–29 Jan**.

## Lines to hold
- **Virgin Active:** "Proposal goes to VA's product team this quarter; Planet Fitness sits down with us next Friday. Both need your price to say yes."
- **Ernie:** "Advanced talks, nothing signed. He captains Team SA at Fancourt."
- **Finance:** "Asset finance structured with RMB, 90/10. Do you have vendor finance?"
- **Revenue share:** "We want Golfzon earning on every shot. Scope first, then we set the number together." *Don't anchor.*
- **Gambling:** "An insured prize contract, not a bet. We carry the compliance, and paid play runs only where permitted."
- **TrackMan:** "We're choosing now, and we'd rather one platform, Golfzon's, across the chain."
