The talk track

Open (60 seconds)

Sean, thanks for making the time. When Golfzon moved global business to Chantilly, it told us where to come. This isn't a dealer conversation.

Quick context. Get Lucky sells golf's most exciting moment, insured: one swing at a par-3 for up to a million rand, with Santam carrying the risk. We're live at 30 courses with 10,000 paid entries.

We're now bringing simulators into South Africa's two biggest gym chains. That's 200 bays proposed to Virgin Active and 72 to Planet Fitness, financed with RMB and operated by us. We're choosing the platform now, and we want it to be Golfzon.

The bays

Three bay types, in three phases, from 2027:

  • TwoVision NX Signature bays in the flagship clubs;
  • Vision Play bays in the metro clubs;
  • GDR Practice bays for coaching.

Planet Fitness sits down with us next Friday, and Virgin Active's product team this quarter. Both need a platform and a price to say yes. That's why you're first.

The turn (answer "Ask us in Virginia")

Here's the part the brief held back. We sell the hole-in-one, insured. Your simulators measure that shot better than any camera on a tee. So we'd like to build it into Golfzon: a Get Lucky mode.

Golfzon locks the tee and the settings and sends us the shot. We run everything else: the wallet, identity checks, the Santam cover, claims and payouts. Every Golfzon bay gets a new paid moment, and the venue and Golfzon both earn on every shot, with no new hardware.

You already do ace pools in Korea. We'd take it everywhere else, with real prizes and the insurance and compliance behind it. We start on our 272 bays, so you see the numbers from your own telemetry first.

Icons

In December, Ernie captains Team South Africa against Olazábal at Fancourt: 28 icons, broadcast to over 150 countries. Get Lucky owns a par-3 there with a R10 million hole-in-one on it. We'd love a Golfzon bay in the fan zone playing that hole. It's the launch moment for the whole thing.

If Ernie comes up (the brief already told him, in confidence):

Still advanced talks, nothing signed. You'll see him at Fancourt.

Close

Can we leave today with a name on your side, a date for pricing and reference designs, and an NDA so we can start scoping the mode? I'll send a note this afternoon with all of it written down.
